# Expense Policy

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 1500 INR per day. A receipt is required for any single expense above 500 INR.

Submit your expense claim within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted later may be rejected. Approved claims are paid back within 10 working days.

For flights shorter than 6 hours, book economy class. Any single expense above 20000 INR needs prior approval from a director before you spend the money.

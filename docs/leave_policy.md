# Leave Policy

Every full-time employee gets 24 days of annual leave per year. Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next year, but the carry forward is limited to a maximum of 5 days. Any extra days expire on 31 March.

Sick leave is 10 days per year. If you are unwell for more than 2 consecutive days, you must submit a doctor's note when you return to work.

Parental leave is 16 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the other parent. Please give your manager at least 5 working days of notice before planned leave, except in emergencies.
